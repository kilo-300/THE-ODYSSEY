TOM HOLLAND


Life

Thomas Stanley Holland (born 1 June 1996) is a British actor known for his
work in a diverse range of genres. His accolades include a BAFTA Award as well as
two Critics' Choice Awards nominations. Holland's films as a leading actor have
grossed over $13.1 billion worldwide, making him one of the highest-grossing
actors of all time. He was featured on the Forbes 30 Under 30 Europe list of 2019.
